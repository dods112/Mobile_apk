About Tan-aw AI

What Tan-aw AI is

Tan-aw AI helps residents of Tandag City check medicine and vaccine availability at health centers before they travel. The tagline is "Check before you go."

How the chatbot works

  • Ask in English or Bisaya, such as "Is amoxicillin available?"
  • Replies show stock status, when it was last confirmed, opening hours, and nearby centers.
  • When an item is unavailable, a referral code may point you to another center for 24 hours.

Data accuracy

Information comes from health center staff. Status older than 24 hours is marked as outdated, and older than 48 hours is marked as stale. Call the center before you travel when information is outdated.

Medical disclaimer

Tan-aw AI is not medical advice and cannot diagnose or prescribe. Availability is not a reservation.

Privacy

Your account, chat history, saved centers, and followed medicines are stored for the app to work. The app does not store medical records.
